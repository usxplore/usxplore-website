How do I choose the right program?

Start with your academic background, preferred subject, career direction and budget. A focused shortlist should balance ambition with realistic entry requirements.

When should I begin my application?

Timelines differ by institution and intake. Starting early gives you more time to prepare documents, meet test requirements and respond to university requests.

What happens after I receive an offer?

Review every condition carefully, confirm tuition and deposit requirements, then prepare the documents needed for your student visa and departure.
