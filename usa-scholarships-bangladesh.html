Common USA funding routes

The right route depends on the study level, university, department and applicant profile. Funding terms should always be confirmed on the university's official website or written offer.

Undergraduate merit scholarships

Universities may consider grades, test scores, leadership and other achievements when awarding partial or substantial merit scholarships.

Graduate merit awards

Some master's and doctoral programs offer departmental scholarships, fellowships or tuition awards based on academic strength and program priorities.

Graduate assistantships

GA appointments may involve academic, administrative or student-support responsibilities, with compensation defined by the university.

Research assistantships

RA opportunities usually depend on faculty research needs, available grants and how closely the applicant's skills match a project.

Teaching assistantships

TA roles may include teaching, grading, tutorials or laboratory support and can require strong subject knowledge and communication skills.

External scholarships

Government, foundation and professional-organization awards may have separate eligibility rules, deadlines and application processes.

Important: “Full funding” can mean different combinations of tuition support, stipend and insurance. Review every offer carefully before making a financial or visa decision.

How to strengthen a scholarship application

  1. Choose universities where your academic record matches the published admission profile.
  2. Check separate scholarship and assistantship deadlines before submitting the admission application.
  3. Prepare a focused CV and statement explaining relevant study, research, leadership and professional experience.
  4. Use recommendations from people who can provide specific evidence of your ability and potential.
  5. For research programs, identify faculty and projects that genuinely match your background.
  6. Compare the remaining tuition, living costs and required financial evidence after any award.

Information needed for an initial assessment

  • SSC, HSC and university academic results, as applicable
  • IELTS, TOEFL or Duolingo score, if available
  • GRE or GMAT score, if available or required
  • Preferred subject, degree level and intake
  • Research, publications, employment and extracurricular experience
  • Estimated annual budget and preferred US states, if any

Frequently asked questions

Can Bangladeshi students receive scholarships in the USA?

Yes. Depending on the institution and program, international students may be considered for merit scholarships, departmental awards or graduate assistantships. Availability and eligibility vary, and funding is never guaranteed.

What is the difference between GA, RA and TA funding?

A graduate assistant may support academic or administrative work, a research assistant supports research projects, and a teaching assistant supports teaching or laboratory duties. Compensation and tuition benefits differ by university and appointment.

When should I apply?

Begin early enough to meet university, scholarship and assistantship deadlines. Funding deadlines can be earlier than general admission deadlines, so each program must be checked individually.
